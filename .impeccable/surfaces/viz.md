Scope: the VIZ surface — bitbounce's second full-screen page: a MIDI-driven generative visual instrument, mounted by the established module-signal + `<Show>` gate pattern (App.tsx:83–85 precedent; a mode of the app, not a URL change). Visitor mode: **Experience** — the visitor is inside the work: the light show leads from the first viewport, three controls recede, and nothing is read or operated beyond jamming. (Mode is a marked assumption, chosen unattended; Operate was considered and declined — the surface has no task state to keep legible beyond playing/stopped, and an instrument-forward reading would put a device between the visitor and the show.)

Audience & job: an existing bitbounce user (casual/prosumer, desktop) with a pattern looping (J1) or stopped (J2), leaning back to watch the song play as light and explore arrangements by casual click — cycle presets, reroll the current one, exit. Never a VJ tool; no export, no show control.

Outcome & proof: every audible note-on lands a visible lane-hued reaction within one frame of its audible time (offset queue at the session.deliverLaneEvents seam; schedule-time events never leak early); reroll yields visibly distinct arrangements and identical seeds reproduce identical arrangements (unit-tested); the audio graph is untouched and the transport never stops across enter/exit. Proof is the surface itself under the densest standard pattern at frame budget.

Chosen direction: **House Lights** — the same Arcade Stage Floor with the grid lifted out. The DAW is the booth looking down at four LED pad floors; VIZ is the house view of the same stage: house lights down, the song runs the rig. One continuous near-black floor (tokens ground), lane hue as the identity of every light event (drums red · bass amber · chords green · lead blue), pitch and intensity shaping each reaction, and a seeded rig of effect nodes — the preset — hung where light happens. Structural laws: light is anchored to hung nodes, never freeform particle soup (the floor is lit, not filled); play raises the stage through cyc-derived density phases (rest → sparse → working → full) as the groove fills; reroll re-hangs the rig live. Color strategy inherited, not new: drenched dark ground, four named lane-hue roles, warm-white ink for info. Scene that forces it: a solo producer at a desk in a dim room, looping a groove, leaning back to watch. No new faces, no new palette values — tokens.css only. (Unattended concept roll key `df36c731`, surface scope, mode experience: dealt hand led by the Orbit Clockwork structure; the cyclorama-fused stage-light structure beat it on both axes — audience identification and product clarity — and supersedes as the recorded direction. Assumption marked, correctable.)

Sequence: enter from the booth → the dark stage holds the rig faintly lit (the arrangement legible as a still diagram) → the next hit ignites lane-hued light where the rig hung it, at audible time → density climbs with the groove → reroll/cycle re-dress the stage → exit returns focus to the booth; the music never interrupts.

Focal moment: the first hit after entry — the dark floor ignites; sustained: a dense four-lane groove holding the stage at full phase.

Implementation consequence: an isolated canvas renderer (the grid stays DOM by design) under the existing rAF laws — direct canvas writes, never framework state; loop paused when the page is hidden; audible-time offset queue keyed to the transport clock; preset/rig model serializable with seeded xorshift32 randomness (`Math.random` banned by the determinism contract); a DOM "remote" chrome carries all a11y; reduced-motion alternative path gated in BOTH CSS and the render loop via matchMedia.

Named raises kept: from the cyclorama challenger (stagecraft theater-lighting): density as light phases, calm-dark to full-light, with named text-equivalent state labels never color alone; from the declined constellation structure: idle legibility — the silent rig reads as a still arrangement, so the preset is legible before it plays; from the declined pyro structure: burst/trail grammar seeds the node vocabulary; from the color-chord challenger: co-incident lane hits may compute their own overlap state (optional production line).

Rejected alternatives (recorded): **Orbit Clockwork** (the dealt lead) — the whole canvas as an orrery, pitch → radius, loop → revolution. Declined as canvas thesis: radial grammar caps the 8–12-preset library at ring variants, and binding revolutions to per-lane pattern lengths re-bases mid-song on quantized switches. Kept as a full preset family (one preset = the orrery) with its discipline: effects may bind to transport time, not wall time. **Lane Rivers** — lanes as four spatial bands with notes as traveling light tokens. Declined for re-tiling the floor into the DAW's own literal lane layout and foreclosing whole-floor compositions; kept its discipline: pitch reads as canonical position within a lane's light field.

World expansion (carried in this brief; no tracked DESIGN.md exists — tokens.css and the route surface brief remain the visual authority): this is a second room of the same world, not a new identity. The tokens.css glow law governs the DOM chrome unchanged (one glow layer, ≤3px blur, 45–60% alpha, ≤180ms trigger decay, no infinite pulses). On-canvas light is content, not UI decoration: the pixel values do not bind, the world rules do — one-shot hit light decays on a bounded envelope (per-preset, production-tunable), nothing pulses forever, the resting state is near-static, and the WCAG three-flash threshold is a hard ceiling (SC 2.3.1). Ink laws apply to all DOM text (warm-white info text on ground; near-black ink on fills; no lane-hued text on fills; red text ≤10px Silkscreen 700 only).

Reroll spam — coalesced with full teardown between arrangements; zero particle/state buildup, zero leaked nodes. Preset switch mid-burst — teardown + mount; transition style open. Page hidden — loop paused; hit policy open (default: drop and resync on visible). Reduced motion — canvas swaps to static placed marks with bounded hold plus textual event equivalence. Phone stage — graceful fallback, form open.

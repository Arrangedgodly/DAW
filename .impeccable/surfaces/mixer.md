# Mixer screen extension

Mode: Operate. Inherit the shipped Bitbounce Membrane palette, type, quiet controls and continuous instrument panels. No new visual identity and no raster assets.

Approved scope: separate Mixer screen; move all existing creative FX from instrument editors; all active tracks up to eight plus master; channel level, pan, mute, solo, lock, EQ, compression; master glue and sample-peak limiter. Auto Mix analyzes locally, previews at matched loudness, applies an undoable proposal, restores previous mix, and rejects stale analysis.

First viewport: shared transport/navigation, clear Mixer title, restrained Auto Mix controls, and channel strips. Selected channel processing follows below. Channel strips and the device rack scroll horizontally within their own bounds, including on phones. Each effect occupies a compact 300px card; its controls use a dense two-column layout. Master has its own addable, reorderable, bypassable and removable creative effects chain before output dynamics. Existing instrument editors retain quick volume/mute/solo but no FX overlays.

Signature interaction: select any channel, edit its saved creative effects and corrective processing, then compare an Auto Mix proposal before applying.

Quality bar: native labeled inputs, keyboard access, stable playback across page changes, readable values, theme tokens, no document overflow at 1440 or 390 px. This is a code-led, precisely scoped extension approved in conversation, with no new concept/comp selection.

Known first-version scope: full linear arrangement analysis up to three minutes; sample-peak limiting rather than true-peak mastering; no automation or per-drum submixing.
